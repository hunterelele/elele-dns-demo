## The data on this site is invented

No household's DNS history is published here. Every device, domain, company,
timestamp and coordinate in the demo is generated by
`scripts/generate-demo-data.mts` from the fixture defined in
`scripts/demo/household.ts`, seeded so that the same commit produces the same
database.

## What the product ships that this does not

The full product enriches live traffic with four datasets, downloaded at image
build or on demand rather than committed. None are present here, because the
demo's attribution and geolocation are written directly by the generator:

- **AdGuard companiesdb** (CC BY-SA 4.0) for tracker and company attribution
- **MaxMind GeoLite2 City** (GeoLite2 EULA; operator-supplied, never redistributed)
- **sapics/ip-location-db** IP-to-country and IP-to-ASN tables
- **IEEE OUI registry** for hardware vendor lookup from a MAC address

If you install the product itself, those terms apply and are reproduced in its
own notices.
